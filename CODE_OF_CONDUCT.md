# Code of conduct

Platty is a small project. Be kind and help keep it a good place to be.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to SSH, Go or open source.
- Criticize code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Keep discussions on topic and in English, so everyone can follow them.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.

## Enforcement

Maintainers can edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and block people who keep breaking them. To report something sensitive, use a [private advisory](https://github.com/PlattySSH/platty/security/advisories/new) so only the maintainers see it.

This applies to every repository in the PlattySSH organization and to any space where you represent the project.
